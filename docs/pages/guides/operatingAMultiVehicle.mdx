# Operating a multi-vehicle

A multi-vehicle is six contracts. Every operator call goes to one of them, and its role is scoped
to that one.

| Call | Contract | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| authorize, configure a vehicle | VehicleManager | `MULTI_VEHICLE_SET_VEHICLE_AUTHORIZATION` |
| thresholds, max total assets | VehicleManager | `MULTI_VEHICLE_SET_THRESHOLDS` |
| queues | QueueStrategyEngine | `MULTI_VEHICLE_SET_QUEUES` |
| dispatch | SectorAccountingEngine | `MULTI_VEHICLE_DISPATCH` |
| move | SectorAccountingEngine | `MULTI_VEHICLE_MOVE` |

A role granted to the multi-vehicle itself is invisible to the engine that checks it. The grant
succeeds and every later call reverts `MissingRole`.

## Allocation

The queues drive automatic allocation. [`buildSetQueuesCall`](/actions/buildSetQueuesCall) sets a
deposit queue and a redeem queue, one target per sub-vehicle, in order. A vehicle in `Manual`
mode, set with [`buildConfigureVehicleCall`](/actions/buildConfigureVehicleCall), cannot sit in a
queue and only moves on an operator dispatch.

## Dispatch and move

[`buildDispatchVehicleCall`](/actions/buildDispatchVehicleCall) deposits into or redeems from one
sub-vehicle. Reallocating between two vehicles is a redeem dispatch followed by a deposit dispatch.
On an async vehicle the redeem does not settle in the transaction. Call `simulateDispatchVehicle`
first when a second leg depends on the first, and keep the returned `query` to progress it.

[`buildMoveBetweenSectorsCall`](/actions/buildMoveBetweenSectorsCall) moves balances between
[sectors](/types/Sector) without touching a sub-vehicle.
[`getSectorBalance`](/actions/getSectorBalance) reads them.

## Limits

[`buildSetThresholdsCall`](/actions/buildSetThresholdsCall) replaces both thresholds at once. Read
[`getVehicleManagerLimits`](/actions/getVehicleManagerLimits) first and pass back the one you are
not changing. [`buildSetMaxTotalAssetsCall`](/actions/buildSetMaxTotalAssetsCall) caps
`totalAssets()` at deposit time; operator flows that grow it without minting shares are exempt.
