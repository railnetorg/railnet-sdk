# Managing roles

Every gated call checks a role on an ExternalAccessControl, for a scope: the contract performing
the call. A role is held globally, for every scope, or scoped to one contract.

## Scoped first

[`buildGrantScopedRoleCall`](/actions/buildGrantScopedRoleCall) confines a role to one contract.
Scope it to the contract that performs the gated call: `MULTI_VEHICLE_SET_QUEUES` to the queue
strategy engine, `CONDUIT_PROCESS` to the conduit. A grant scoped to the wrong contract succeeds,
and the gated call reverts `MissingRole` with `[role, scope, caller]`, so the scope it checked is
in the error.

The caller of a scoped grant holds the role's admin role, `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` unless it was
reassigned, either globally or scoped to the same scope. A scoped role that is public reverts
`PublicRoleAuthDenied` on grant and revoke;
[`buildSetScopedRolePublicCall`](/actions/buildSetScopedRolePublicCall) turns it off first.

## Global

[`buildGrantRoleCall`](/actions/globalRoles) applies to every scope. It takes a base role,
`keccak256(name)`, the form the [role constants](/constants/roles) export. A value already encoded
against a scope, `keccak256(role, scope)`, has the same shape. `grantRole` would store it as a
base role, and no client-side check can tell the two apart.

## Checking

[`getHasRole`](/actions/getHasRole) answers the question the contract asks: may this account act
under this role for this scope, globally or scoped. A public role reads as held by every account.
[`useHasRole`](/react/useHasRole) is the hook.
