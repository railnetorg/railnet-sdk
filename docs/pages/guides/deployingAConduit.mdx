# Deploying a conduit

A conduit spawn is one transaction against the ConduitFactory. The factory needs four modules,
an authorized asset and an approval before it accepts the call.

## Before the spawn

1. **Modules.** `feeManager`, `accountList`, `ownerRegistry` and `accessControl` must each come
   from the factory this ConduitFactory trusts for that module. Spawn them with
   [`buildSpawnFeeManagerCall`](/actions/buildSpawnFeeManagerCall),
   [`buildSpawnAccountListCall`](/actions/buildSpawnAccountListCall),
   [`buildSpawnOwnerRegistryCall`](/actions/buildSpawnOwnerRegistryCall) and
   [`buildSpawnAccessControlCall`](/actions/buildSpawnAccessControlCall), or reuse existing ones.
   A module from anywhere else reverts `NotDeployedByFactory`.
2. **Role.** The caller needs `CONDUIT_SPAWN` on the factory's access control. The other
   factories gate their spawn behind `FACTORY_SPAWN`; this one does not.
3. **The initial deposit.** The factory pulls a seed deposit of the vehicle's asset from the
   caller. Read the amount with [`getInitialDepositAmount`](/actions/getInitialDepositAmount),
   approve the factory for at least that much, and hold at least that much. The amount is
   registry state, not a parameter of the spawn.

## The spawn

[`buildSpawnConduitCall`](/actions/buildSpawnConduitCall) takes the modules, the vehicle, the
ERC-20 metadata and two salts from [`randomSalt`](/utilities/randomSalt). `initialExpectedSupply`
must be non-zero.

The address is a CREATE2 over the proxy's init code, which carries every field of the call.
[`predictConduitDeployment`](/actions/predictConduitDeployment) resolves it ahead of time from
exactly the parameters you will send. After the transaction,
[`extractConduitAddress`](/utilities/receiptHelpers) reads it from the receipt.

## After the spawn

The conduit stays disabled until the seed deposit settles. On a synchronous vehicle that happens
inside `spawn()`. On an async vehicle, send
[`buildFinalizeConduitDepositCall`](/actions/buildFinalizeConduitDepositCall) once the vehicle has
settled.

Transfers of conduit shares start in the state `transferEnabled` set.
[`buildEnableConduitTransfersCall`](/actions/buildEnableConduitTransfersCall) turns them on. No
call turns them back off.
